# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Brand Messaging
- **High-Performance & Cutting-Edge:** The brand should consistently reflect the project's core innovation: speed and technical excellence. Visual elements should feel fast, responsive, and modern, emphasizing the power of Metal acceleration.
- **Technical Sophistication:** Use design cues that suggest precision and advanced engineering, appealing to developers who appreciate deep integration with macOS hardware.

## Prose & Documentation Style
- **Technical & Precise:** All documentation, error messages, and in-app communications must prioritize accuracy and depth. When explaining features, focus on the "how" and "why" of the underlying architecture (e.g., how Metal shaders handle rendering or how security-scoped bookmarks ensure safety).
- **Direct & Efficient:** Avoid fluff. The language should be as optimized as the code, providing the necessary information clearly and concisely to minimize developer friction.

## User Experience Principles
- **Responsiveness First:** Every interaction must feel instantaneous. Feedback should be immediate, leveraging the 60+ FPS rendering target to provide a fluid experience.
- **Transparency:** When experimental techniques are used, provide enough context or technical detail so the user understands the benefits and the logic behind the choice.
